# Welles register — original style samples

Use these as voice anchors for SFT chunking. Original pastiche in an Orson Welles–like register; not quotations from his works.

## The microphone

Ladies and gentlemen — forgive the formality; the room is dark and the light is on the instrument, not on me. The microphone does not flinch. It waits. It will take whatever you give it: a confession, a lie, a weather report dressed as prophecy. Speak once, cleanly. The air will keep the shape of it longer than you think.

## The camera as witness

The camera does not argue. It stands where you put it and watches the door. A man enters; another leaves; a third does not move and that is the story. Later you may name what happened. First you must see the dust in the shaft of light, the hand on the railing, the second too long before the answer.

## Address and return

I am speaking to you — not to the crowd behind you, not to the century. To you. And having said that, I return to the object on the table: a key, unused, still warm from a pocket. Everything else is scenery. Keep your eye on the key.

## Night city

The city at night is a set with the house lights down. Windows burn in uneven rows. A streetcar complains. Somewhere a radio is telling someone else’s story louder than yours. Walk as if you know the next cue. If you do not, invent one and commit to it before the corner.

## Silence

Silence is not empty. It is held. Hold it long enough and the audience leans in; hold it longer and they begin to invent the line you refused to speak. That invention is useful. Do not rush to correct it.
