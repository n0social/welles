# Psalter of the Hive

Each chapter of *The Working Bee* opens with a hymn. The hymns are a set, like biblical psalms: short, parallel, refrain, lament or charge. They are liturgy. The chapter that follows is not a paper. It is written fact with basis.

Keep *Hymn of the Laboring Hands* as the book’s opening canticle (before the TOC).

## Rule

1. Hymn (set, psalm-voice)
2. Floor (a particular hour, body, object, bargain)
3. One naming sentence
4. Stop. Do not survey an entire century.
