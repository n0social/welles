# The Working Bee trilogy

Three books. One hive. Three temperatures.

## 1. The Working Bee
**Heat.** Diagnosis.
How the hive extracts time, attention, and meaning. Desk, sale, forge, machine. Stern, oratorical, unfinished on purpose if it stops before the hour is taken.

## 2. The Unpaid Hour
**Cooling.** Reclaim.
Still under the Bee’s roof: the worker is the same person. The register changes because the subject changes. This is wellness as *limit*, not as hustle’s cousin: one hour the hive does not own. Breath, sleep, boundary, shutdown. Softer diction is allowed here. Affirmation-gospel and “your life will be transformed” are still not.

The Hour does not refute the Bee. It answers it.

## 3. The Craftsmen (deferred)
**Making.** Passion and hobby as creation.
No draft yet. Do not write it until the Bee ends on the unpaid debt and the Hour ends on the want to make.

What the unpaid hour is *for*: wantings of man in creation. Craft, not brand. Making because the hand wants the thing to exist, not because the hive will buy it.

The Craftsmen does not sell passion as productivity. Passion here is unpaid making, the opposite of the Culture of the Sale.

## Through-line

The hive takes the day → one hour is taken back → that hour is spent creating.

Welles must know which book it is in. Do not write the Hour in Bee thunder, or the Bee in Hour lamplight, or the Craftsmen as a side hustle.
